The Effect of the Computer Revolution: is it Good or Bad

Introduction

The computer revolution changed the world in more ways than any of us can ever know. It's revolutionized countless industries, created more than a few industries, and affected us in ways we may never know. Now I won't sit here and spew any garbage about how the computer revolution never caused any problems, because it did. What I'll tell you instead, is that while the computer revolution has, in fact, caused many problems over the years, the ways that it has changed the world for the better far outnumber the ways that it has changed the world for the worse. For brevity sake, this article will only be going over a few examples of the good and bad effects of the computer revolution.

The Pros

Let's start things off on a high note here with the pros of the computer revolution. The first thing that comes to mind is automation, thanks to the computer revolution it is possible to automate almost anything which increases both speed and efficiency in most situations. The second thing that comes to mind is communications and information distribution. The growth of communications has had more positive effects on the world than I can count, it's had positive effects on everything from business, to politics, to peoples personal lives. And finally, the evolution of information distribution technologies has had just as many pros as the growth of communications technology, if not more. the growth of information distribution technology not only helps with most of the same things as communication technology, it even helps with industries such as education.

The Cons

while the computer revolution has had it's benifits, it's also had a lot of drawbacks. One example of this is the fact that now it's possible for someone to steal another person's identity from the other side of the planet. Another drawback of the computer revolution is unfortunately social media, while social media has a lot of upsides, it also has plenty of downsides, as it enhances the spread of misinformation and also enables cyberbullying. Additionally, it has also given rise to hacking, and the issues with that are far reaching.
